Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026  ·  shareevent.vercel.app

Short version: We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything happens in your browser. We have no servers, no database, no analytics.

1. Who We Are

Event Share Generator is a browser-based tool that helps event attendees create and share personalised graphics on LinkedIn. It is operated as a client-side web application with no backend infrastructure.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, title, or company
  • Photos you upload
  • Captions or text you write
  • IP addresses or device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Cookies (we set none)

3. What Stays on Your Device

The following data is stored only in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device:

  • Event organiser configuration (event name, date, location, colours, logo, caption template)

This data is saved so the organiser does not have to re-enter settings on each visit. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

Photos you upload are loaded into browser memory for the session only and are not persisted anywhere after you close the tab.

4. Image Generation

All graphic creation happens entirely in your browser using the dom-to-image-more library. No image data is sent to any server. The generated PNG is saved directly to your device's Downloads folder or clipboard.

5. LinkedIn

When you click "Open LinkedIn", you are redirected to LinkedIn's website. From that point, LinkedIn's own Privacy Policy applies. We do not have a LinkedIn integration, OAuth connection, or any access to your LinkedIn account. We cannot post on your behalf.

6. Third-Party Services

We load the Inter font from Google Fonts (fonts.google.com). This means Google's servers receive your IP address when the font is fetched. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. No other third-party services are used.

7. Children's Privacy

This tool is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the tool after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at the contact details provided on the event's official website.
